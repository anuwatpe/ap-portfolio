About ONTHAI Lab

Research Laboratory

Lab Identifier ONTHAI LAB

Faculty of Public Health and Allied Health Sciences, Praboromarajchanok Institute, Ministry of Public Health.

ONTHAI Lab brings together researchers, students, and collaborators to conduct evidence synthesis and develop trustworthy health data, biomedical ontologies, health AI, and digital tools for digital and population health.

Active Lab Research

Current Lab Projects & Initiatives

Ongoing evidence synthesis, biomedical ontology development, health AI, and digital health initiatives at ONTHAI Lab.

ONTHAI Lab Project Ongoing

Biomedical Ontology for Cholangiocarcinoma (CCA)

Expanding cholangiocarcinoma ontology coverage to include genetic risk factors, biomarker associations, and integration with national Thai cancer registries.

ONTHAI Lab Project Ongoing

Digital Health Literacy for Community Health Workers

Developing and evaluating digital health competency frameworks and training modules for public health practitioners in rural Thailand.

ONTHAI Lab Project Ongoing

Public Health and Epidemiological Informatics & GIS for Disease Surveillance

Spatial analysis and predictive modeling for communicable disease surveillance using GIS and health information system data in Northeast Thailand.
